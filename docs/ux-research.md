# Calculator Ecosystem UX Research

Research completed before implementation on 17 August 2026. This is a product-pattern review, not a visual reference to copy.

## Sources reviewed

- Groww calculator directory: https://groww.in/calculators
- ClearTax EMI calculator: https://cleartax.in/s/emi-calculator
- SEBI Investor calculator directory: https://investor.sebi.gov.in/calculators/index.html
- Independent Indian calculator products were sampled to understand common search, formatting and category patterns.

## Useful product patterns

### Groww

- A broad calculator inventory benefits from plain, familiar calculator names.
- Related calculators support lateral discovery once a user enters through one tool.
- Product-specific variants such as home-loan EMI can coexist with a general EMI tool.

**Applied to FInova:** structured metadata, seven plain-language categories, aliases for search, and a compact popular set instead of an overwhelming homepage directory.

### ClearTax

- A useful result should be followed by a calculation breakdown, formula and realistic explanation.
- Financial tools benefit from detailed schedules where the problem requires them, such as amortisation.
- Tax tools need rule periods and update context.

**Reserved for future calculator pages:** breakdowns, schedules, formula explanations, source dates and rule-year labels. No calculator logic was built in this phase.

### SEBI Investor

- A serious ecosystem extends beyond EMI and SIP into net worth, insurance needs, goal planning, cost of delay, compounding and retirement.
- Categories are necessary as breadth grows.
- Educational framing improves understanding of less familiar tools.

**Applied to FInova:** scalable categories and catalogue metadata include planning and net-worth concepts, while the guide preview establishes an educational layer.

## Deliberate departures

- No brokerage, product-selling or account-opening prompts.
- No invented popularity statistics or trust badges.
- No bank-like visual language.
- No dense wall of calculator links above the fold.
- No copied layouts, colours, illustrations, content or component designs.

FInova’s identity comes from editorial typography, numbered folios, ledger-like rules, tabular figures and a restrained neutral/teal palette.
